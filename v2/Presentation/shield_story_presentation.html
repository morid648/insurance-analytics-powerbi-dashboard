The Answer

So — Was March a Fluke, or a Signal?

The data gives a clear answer. And it points to 5 specific actions.

The March spike was real momentum. But the business has structural vulnerabilities that need addressing before the next spike can be sustained.

1
Identify the March growth driver — campaign, seasonal, or channel-led? Replicate if possible.
2
Double down on online channels — equal revenue/customer (₹37.16K) with 2× better resilience.
3
Expand into secondary cities — Chennai (₹106M) and Indore (₹81M) show consistent demand.
4
Segment underwriting by age — risk climbs from 39% to 72%; product pricing must reflect this.
5
Build a post-peak acquisition pipeline — acquisition fell from 7.1K to 4.1K in a single month.
